Why solar irrigation?

Lower running costs

Once installed, the sun powers your pump. No diesel, no Eskom bills and far less maintenance.

Reliable water security

Pump and store water on your own schedule, so a dry spell does not wipe out a season's yield.

Built for small farms

Modular kits start small and grow with you, with local support and clear advice in plain language.
