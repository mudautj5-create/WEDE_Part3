About Kunyarungo AgriTech Solutions

A rural agritech enterprise making solar water technology affordable for the farmers who need it most.

Who we are and what we stand for

Kunyarungo AgriTech Solutions began in a rural farming region of South Africa, where small scale farmers were losing crops to unpredictable rainfall while diesel and electricity costs kept climbing. We set out to design solar powered irrigation and water management systems that cost less to run and are simple enough to install, understand and maintain.

Today we supply kits and consultation services to emerging farmers, agricultural cooperatives and funded community projects.

To empower small scale and emerging farmers with affordable, sustainable agricultural technology that improves crop yield and water security.

To become a leading provider of accessible, renewable energy agricultural solutions across Southern Africa.
